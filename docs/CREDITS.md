# Footage credits

Every animal's motion was studied from footage of the real animal: clips and
papers were watched, tracked and measured, and the build was tuned until it
measured the same. The footage was used **for measurement only**. No frame of
any clip is shipped on the site, in this repository or in the `critter-kit`
package, and none shaped a habitat or a look.

Thank you to the people who filmed these animals and shared the film under
open licences. Without it there would be nothing true to measure.

## Frogs

Movement evidence from the literature, described in
[`FROG_FOOTAGE_LOG.md`](FROG_FOOTAGE_LOG.md) with what each source supports:
Song et al. 2024, _Journal of Experimental Biology_ 227, jeb246931 (tree frogs
landing); Richards 2010, _Journal of Experimental Biology_ 213, 621–632
(swimming with the hind limbs); and the other sources listed there.
