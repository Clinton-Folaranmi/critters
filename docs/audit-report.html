Frog pond study: audit report

An end-to-end audit of /play/frogs, /play/frogs/day and both standalone builds. Nothing in the product was changed. Each finding below was reproduced and measured; fixes wait on Clinton's approval.

Working tree on master at 2bf1a18 plus uncommitted work (sound, bank, shore, switch). Headless Chromium shell on the M1 over CDP with real input events; world soaks frame-stepped in Node. Machine load stayed at 3.3–4.1 throughout.

1 major 13 minor 12 nits 0 blockers

The short version

  • The simulation is sound. world.check() stayed empty through 4.5 hours of simulated time at both aspects (untouched, tapped and resizing). No frog body ever reached the bank, no pad rim went under it, and no state got stuck.
  • The faint horizontal lines over the pond come from the site's paper overlay, .paper-surface::after. It is fixed at z-index 1000 and draws over the pond. Neither renderer causes them.
  • Lifecycle is clean: one canvas at a time, one AudioContext per mount (every earlier one closed), and the heap levels off after 80 Dusk/Noon switches.
  • The main problems are at the edges: the standalone files have no doctype or viewport tag; taps in the masked-out corners still act; the focus ring doesn't follow the pond; the Canvas renderer casts midge shadows toward the sun; swimmers' legs can slip under the bank on phones.
  • GPU cost couldn't be measured reliably on this machine. Paired runs put the working tree level with HEAD within noise.

Findings, most severe first

  1. MajorStandalone

    Standalone pages render in quirks mode with no mobile viewport

    standalone/frogs/page.html:1 · standalone/frogs-day/page.html:1

    What
    Both templates start at <title>. They have no <!doctype html>, no <html lang>, no <meta charset> and no viewport meta.
    Evidence
    Opened from file://, both report document.compatMode = "BackCompat" and characterSet = "windows-1252", and neither has a viewport meta. On a phone the page lays out at the default 980 px viewport, so the max-width: 640px layout and .91 aspect never apply when the file is opened directly. The body's default 8 px margin also leaves a strip round the dark dusk page.
    Fix
    Add a doctype, lang, charset and width=device-width, initial-scale=1 to both templates, and zero the body margin. The build script could check for these the way it checks for the bundle marker.
  2. MinorInteraction

    Taps in the masked-out margin still act

    components/frogs/frog-scene.ts:174 · frog-bank.ts:115 (mask) · globals.css:248

    What
    The ragged edge is a CSS mask-image, which doesn't affect hit-testing. The specimen-plate round clipped taps with clip-path; the bank rounds dropped that.
    Evidence
    With the mask rasterised, alpha is below 0.05 on 17.7% of the canvas at 880×595 and 19.9% at 375×412. Real clicks at the top-right, bottom-right and top-left canvas corners, where nothing is visible, each produced tap-water (a plop, plus a ripple that starts out of sight).
    Fix
    In onPointerDown, ignore taps outside the same superellipse bankMaskSvg draws (it's pure maths), or add a matching clip-path.
  3. MinorAccessibility

    The pond's focus ring cuts through the picture and nearly vanishes at dusk

    app/globals.css:270–287 · standalone/*/page.html:61–74

    What
    The ring is a plain ellipse (inset: 3% 2.5%, 50% radius) in --study-ink. The picture's edge is a squarish superellipse, so the ring crosses the grass on the left and the pads on the right, and the bottom-right corner of the picture sits outside it. The comment says it sits "outside the masked picture", which isn't true.
    Evidence
    Keyboard focus screenshots, both lights. At dusk the dark 2 px ring over dark water shows only where it crosses the paper. The :has() fallback outline sits 18 px inside the canvas, where the mask hides it at the corners.
    Fix
    Draw the ring from the same superellipse as the mask (an SVG outline or a second mask), a little outside it, with a light halo so it reads over dark water.
  4. MinorRenderer parity · Light

    Canvas fallback casts midge shadows toward the sun

    components/frogs/frog-canvas.ts:863–864

    What
    x − away[0]·h and y − away[1]·h use the wrong sign. away already points away from the light, so the shadow lands on the sunward side. The shader (frog-shaders.ts:760) is right.
    Evidence
    One midge placed over open water at height 0.08, frame drawn with and without it. The shadow centroid is at (−13.3, +25.0) px in WebGL (down-left, correct) and (+12.4, −25.9) px in Canvas (up-right, toward the light).
    Fix
    Add away · height · 0.6 instead of subtracting it, so the Canvas shadow matches the shader's offset.
  5. MinorWorld · Visual

    Swimming frogs' legs slip under the bank

    components/frogs/frog-world.ts:483–486 (pondDistance) · 1446–1447 (contain)

    What
    Containment keeps the body centre ≥ 0.8 body lengths + 0.012 off the shore. A frog turning back from the bank trails its hind legs about 1.5 body lengths behind it, onto the land. The bank layer is drawn over swimmers, so the legs disappear under the grass.
    Evidence
    Frame-stepped soaks with the frog's drawn shape sampled on a 21×21 grid: the body never reached land. Legs or toes did: up to 0.015 world units deep (60 min untouched, aspect 1.48) and 0.049 (30 min tapped, aspect 0.91, about 20 px at 375 px wide). The worst frame, replayed in the browser, is below.
    Fix
    Measure containment against the trailing foot as well as the centre (for example, sample the hip-to-toe line) or push the bank clearance for swimmers up to about 1.5 body lengths.
  6. MinorVisual · Site CSS

    The faint horizontal lines over the pond are the site's paper fibres

    app/globals.css:99–127 (.paper-surface::before / ::after)

    What
    .paper-surface::after is position: fixed; z-index: 1000; opacity: .15, with repeating-linear-gradient(2deg …) light lines every ~109 px and 93° dark lines. It is drawn over everything, the pond included. ::before adds multiply grain on top as well.
    Evidence
    The lines appear in identical places in WebGL and ?renderer=canvas, even crossing pads. The raw canvas pixels (toDataURL) have none. With .paper-surface::after hidden they are gone (below). The overlay also nudges measured page brightness.
    Fix
    This is the global paper surface, which earlier briefs said not to touch, so it's Clinton's call. Options: give .animal-study__stage its own stacking context above the overlay, or mask the overlay out over the stage.
  7. MinorPerformance

    Bank re-bake is a throttle, not a debounce, and every bake blocks the main thread

    components/frogs/frog-webgl.ts:139–148 · frog-canvas.ts:1010–1017 · frog-bank-layer.ts:65

    What
    The timer is set once and never reset (&& !bankTimer), so during a continuous resize it re-bakes about every 250 ms rather than once 200 ms after resizing stops. Each bake is synchronous: the first frame, every Dusk/Noon switch and every renderer swap all bake again. The Canvas and WebGL renderers each bake their own copy.
    Evidence
    Bake time (3 runs each): 154–187 ms at 1520×1028, 208–261 ms at 1760×1190 (the real Retina size of the 880 px stage), 20–30 ms at 375×412. A 2.3 s drag-resize produced 6 bakes in WebGL and 6 in Canvas (long tasks of 50–64 ms at those sizes). Each context-loss swap added another 54–69 ms.
    Fix
    Reset the timer on every size change so it only bakes once resizing stops. Cache the baked layer by width, height and theme in frog-bank-layer.ts so a renderer swap reuses it.
  8. MinorSound

    A remembered "on" needs two taps to start on touch screens

    components/frogs/frog-scene.ts:94–107

    What
    The deferred start listens for pointerdown. For touch, Chrome grants user activation on pointerup/touchend, so hasBeenActive is still false at the first pointerdown. The handler re-arms and returns. iOS Safari only unlocks audio in touchend/click, so it may not start at all (not verified on hardware).
    Evidence
    Emulated touch, sound stored "on": the event trace was pointerdown:touch:false → pointerup:touch:true. No AudioContext after the first tap; one running after the second. With a mouse it starts on the first click.
    Fix
    Also listen for pointerup and click (capture), and start on whichever comes first once activation is granted.
  9. MinorSound · Tooling

    The dev peak meter under-reads by 2–3 dB

    components/frogs/frog-audio.ts:674–681

    What
    tick() runs every 100 ms and reads one 2048-sample window (≈43 ms at 48 kHz), so it sees less than half the signal. The guide's "−11 to −13 dBFS" was measured this way.
    Evidence
    60 s real-time tapped soaks with a full-coverage tap on the output: dusk meter −11.9 vs true −9.0 dBFS; noon meter −10.6 vs true −8.4 dBFS. No samples above 0.99, so no clipping.
    Fix
    Take the meter's peak from an AudioWorklet or a maxDecibels-style running max per render quantum, or at least make the window cover the tick interval. Then update the documented figure.
  10. MinorLight

    Noon is now below its brightness target

    components/frogs/frog-theme.ts:94–125 (day palette)

    What
    The bank puts darker earth and grass into the frame. Noon has dropped under the 85–90 target that matched it to the koi study.
    Evidence
    Mean luma inside the visible picture (mask > 0.5): Noon WebGL 82.7, Canvas 80.4 (p10–p90 57–108 / 55–106). Dusk WebGL 39.2, Canvas 36.6 (target 35–40 ✓; p90 66 against ≈57). Renderer gaps 2.3–2.6, within the 5-point rule.
    Fix
    Lift the day bank palette (soil*, grass*) or the water slightly and re-measure. Or restate the target as "water only".
  11. MinorComposition

    Stage pads overlap heavily at the phone aspect

    components/frogs/frog-world.ts:227–236 (PADS)

    What
    Pad positions are shares of width, so at aspect 0.91 the middle stages crowd together. The handoff calls this "slight".
    Evidence
    Rim-to-rim at rest, aspect 0.91: pads 2–3 overlap by 0.080 world units (70% of pad 3's radius), pads 0–3 by 0.038. At 1.48, 0–3 are 0.008 apart. Visible in the 375 px screenshots.
    Fix
    Give PADS a second layout for narrow aspects, or a light pad-to-pad separation in stepPads.
  12. MinorInteraction

    A tap on the bank shows nothing

    components/frogs/frog-world.ts:1704–1708 · frog-scene.ts:165–173

    What
    Bank taps return 'bank' and emit tap-bank, with no ripple and no visual change. With sound off (the default) a bank tap does nothing visible. The field manual asks that every tap produce a visible response.
    Evidence
    __frogTap on land → 'bank', one tap-bank sound event, ripples unchanged.
    Fix
    A small local response: a grass stir baked as a short overlay, a few fireflies or midges lifting off, or a tiny ripple at the waterline when the tap is close to it.
  13. MinorBehaviour · Docs

    Swims run longer, and hops come more often, than the motion sheet says

    components/frogs/frog-world.ts:1286, 1321–1334 · FROG_ART_DIRECTION.md:150, 153

    What
    After swimFor (3–7 s) a frog may still be heading away. It can only turn in proportion to distance travelled, so coming round at glide speed takes several seconds.
    Evidence
    2 h untouched per aspect: swim median 8.1 s (1.48) / 5.5 s (0.91), p90 14.3 / 11.0 s, max 24.8 / 28.2 s. Pad jumps 2.64–2.66 per frog per minute (about 6 across the pond in 45 s; the sheet says 0–3); 28–29% end in the water (sheet: about a quarter ✓). With taps, one swim reached 49.7 s because each dive restarts the timer.
    Fix
    Decide which is right. Either update the sheet, or start steering toward the chosen pad a second or two before swimFor ends.
  14. MinorRenderer parity

    Canvas caustics read as a regular cell grid

    components/frogs/frog-canvas.ts:294–310 vs frog-shaders.ts:282–288, 332–335

    What
    The shader warps the two caustic copies with the surface and scales them by depth and by shade under pads. Canvas tiles the raw cells at a uniform 17% with neither, so at Noon the fallback shows a crisp polygon net across the whole pond.
    Evidence
    1520 px stills of both renderers at Noon, side by side. The Canvas web is visibly tiled and stronger in deep water.
    Fix
    Fade the Canvas caustics with the column layer's depth (or a baked depth mask) and soften the tile, for example with a blurred copy.
  15. NitInteraction

    Taps on overhanging grass and on reeds count as water

    components/frogs/frog-world.ts:1705

    Evidence
    1,045 opaque bank pixels at 880×595 (345 at 375×412) sit where shoreDistance > 0. Tapping there gives a plop and a ripple under the grass. The reeds aren't hit-tested at all.
    Fix
    Test taps against the baked bank's alpha (the layer is already in memory) before the shore distance.
  16. NitLight

    Reeds cast no shadow

    frog-shaders.ts:695–742 · frog-canvas.ts:889–962

    Evidence
    Everything else (frogs, pads, stones, grass blades, midges) has a down-left shadow. The reeds sit on the bank and over the water with none.
    Fix
    Draw each blade a second time, offset down-left at low alpha, before the blade itself.
  17. NitRenderer parity

    Small constants that don't match between renderers

    frog-canvas.ts:381 vs frog-shaders.ts:786 · frog-canvas.ts:953 vs frog-shaders.ts:740 · frog-canvas.ts:964–975 vs frog-shaders.ts:828–830

    Evidence
    Pad contact-shadow strength is .32/.50 in Canvas and .30/.45 in the shader. Day seed heads are #4a3319 in Canvas but the shader uses the dusk brown in both lights, and head length is 3.7× width vs 3.4×. The vignette is a linear ramp in Canvas and a smoothstep in the shader.
    Fix
    Move these into FROG_PALETTES so both renderers read one value.

  19. NitCode quality

    Duplicated helpers, colours and copy outside their modules

    frog-caustics.ts:4 · frog-canvas.ts:36, 135–141 · frog-bank-layer.ts:19, 57 · frog-shaders.ts:99–104, 329, 347 · standalone/*/page.html:176–202 · frog-pond-study.tsx:69–70

    Evidence
    seeded() is copied 3×, smooth() 4×. Canvas depthAt() repeats the shader's pondDepth() constants (.24, .72, .28, 1.2, .55) by hand. The glow centre (aspect·.84, 1.02) appears three times. The field manual asks for every colour in the palette module; the renderers still hold literals (firefly, ripple, tongue, flowers, frog LOOKS duplicated from frogSkin(), which do currently match). The standalone pages hard-code their own lede and instructions, which differ from FROG_COPY, and the React instructions string isn't in FROG_COPY either.
    Fix
    Put the depth field in frog-bank.ts (JS plus generated GLSL, like BANK_GLSL), share one small maths helper, and move copy and light-dependent colours into frog-theme.ts.
  20. NitBank layer

    A quarter of the waterline pebbles never get placed

    components/frogs/frog-bank-layer.ts:166–174, 206–212

    Evidence
    pick(-0.004, 0.016) hits the thin band with p = 0.022 (1.48) / 0.026 (0.91), so 60 tries fail 26% / 21% of the time: about 66 of 252 pebbles are missing at 1.48 and 32 of 155 at 0.91. The loop is bounded (no hang).
    Fix
    Place waterline items by walking the shore curve (bottomBank/leftBank) rather than by rejection sampling.
  21. NitBank layer

    The bank rearranges itself on every resize

    components/frogs/frog-bank-layer.ts:164, 206–316

    Evidence
    Loop counts scale with aspect (170 * aspect etc.) and points are drawn as random() * aspect, so the seeded sequence shifts. Comparing bakes of the left 400 px: 0% of bank pixels change at the same size, 1.5% after +1 px width, 13.1% after +10 px. Grass and stones visibly jump when the re-bake lands.
    Fix
    Seed each item from its own index, or place items in shore-relative coordinates, so a resize moves them rather than re-rolling them.
  22. NitSound

    Two paths create an AudioContext nobody asked for

    components/frogs/frog-scene.ts:103

    Evidence
    With "on" remembered, the first pointerdown is caught in capture. If that press is on the Sound button to turn it off, a context is created and then suspended (seen: made: 1, suspended). Where navigator.userActivation is missing (older Safari/Firefox), ?? true creates the context at mount with no gesture. That context starts suspended and isn't resumed by the first tap. This second path wasn't verified in those browsers.
    Fix
    Skip the deferred start when the gesture target is the Sound button. Treat a missing userActivation as "not yet active" and wait for a gesture.
  23. NitPerformance

    Small per-frame garbage

    components/frogs/frog-webgl.ts:150–171 · frog-world.ts:1510–1511, 1540–1551

    Evidence
    world.step allocates about 2.2 KB per frame (Node, GC off). Contributors: [1, -1] literals, limbEnd tuples in the 12-step bisection, freePads() maps. WebGL draw() adds about 55 small arrays through .set([...]), plus the reedRoots() tuple (≈350 B). The heap stays flat, so this is GC churn, not a leak.
    Fix
    Write uniforms by index into the typed arrays and hoist the constant arrays. Cache reedRoots per aspect.
  24. NitCode quality

    Exports nothing else uses, and generic route titles

    frog-bank.ts:27–28 · frog-world.ts:14–15 · app/play/frogs/page.tsx:5 · app/play/frogs/day/page.tsx:5

    Evidence

    Fix
    Drop the exports. Build metadata from FROG_COPY.

What I couldn't verify

  • How it sounds. Levels, counts, caps and node lifetimes are measured. Nobody has listened yet.
  • GPU cost on a quiet machine or in a visible pane. The load average was 3.3–4.1; the readPixels method gave bimodal samples, and timer queries were inflated about 2.5×. Only the paired comparison with HEAD is meaningful.
  • Real touch hardware and iOS Safari audio unlock. Touch was emulated through CDP.
  • Safari and Firefox (:has(), mask-image hit-testing, navigator.userActivation, autoplay rules).
  • A real tab switch and a real OS reduced-motion toggle. Hide/show was simulated by overriding document.hidden; reduced motion was emulated with Emulation.setEmulatedMedia.
  • Long real-time watching. Real-time runs were 60 s per light; everything longer was frame-stepped.
  • The standalone files as claude.ai artifacts, and on a real phone.
  • A low-end GPU.
Measurement scripts (CDP driver, soaks, parity, levels) are in the audit session's scratchpad, not the repo. No product files were changed, nothing was staged or committed, and unrelated working-tree changes were left alone.
